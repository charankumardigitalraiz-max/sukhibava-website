Our Journey

Our Story

JIA NATURALS is a cold-pressed edible oil manufacturing enterprise focused on traditional mechanical extraction and natural processing.

The Ghani way, with careful processing.

Our framework centers on Zero Heat Induction, Zero Chemical Refining and Natural Clarification. The oil is naturally settled through gravitational sedimentation and then passed through cold micro-fabric filtration.

Our extraction setup uses 2 × VOC 20 KG Automatic Oil Extraction Ghani Machines powered by IE-4 motors, manufactured by Zen Tech Engineering, Coimbatore.
